# EXAMGEN AI — agreed domain modules

The product roadmap is organised into these ten modules, in this exact order:

1. **Content ingestion** — import PDFs, documents and previous tests.
2. **Subject intelligence** — identify subjects, topics, concepts, learning outcomes and examinable material.
3. **Examination blueprint** — define marks, topic weightings, difficulty and cognitive demand.
4. **Visual intelligence** — identify where diagrams or images are academically necessary and request approval before generation or integration.
5. **Question generation** — produce structured, varied and syllabus-aligned questions.
6. **Examination website** — configure, take, submit and review practice examinations and formal simulations.
7. **Marking and moderation** — automatically mark objective questions and apply rubrics to written responses, with moderation controls.
8. **Question bank** — store, classify, reuse and track question provenance.
9. **Adaptive learning** — analyse performance and adjust difficulty and revision priorities.
10. **Academic resource production** — produce examination papers, memoranda, examiner notes and revision PDFs.

## Implementation discipline

The ten modules remain the agreed product scope and order. Phase 1 establishes the examination-engine foundation across Modules 3, 5, 6 and 7; it does not remove, rename or reorder the other modules. Content ingestion, subject intelligence, visual intelligence, question-bank lifecycle, adaptive learning and academic resource production remain planned until separately implemented and tested.

Student accounts are intentionally deferred. The current attempt identifier is a temporary bearer capability for this MVP and must not be treated as account authentication.
